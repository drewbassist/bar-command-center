Bar Cycle

Every two weeks, one subject becomes the focus. Every day, all thirteen subjects remain active through essays, mixed MCQs, active recall, and spaced repetition.

2-Week Focus Cycle What Happens During These 14 Days
Focus Subject Learn the doctrine, understand the concepts, build or update your outline, create flashcards, and complete subject-specific essays and MCQs.
Daily Essay Write one essay every day, rotating through all thirteen bar subjects. Every subject is practiced at least once during each two-week cycle.
Mixed MCQs Complete 20–25 mixed MCQs daily using interleaving. Most questions come from previously completed subjects while gradually increasing questions from the current focus subject.
Active Recall Recall rule statements before looking them up. Rewrite missed rules from essays and MCQs.
Spaced Repetition Review flashcards, previous essays, and missed MCQs according to your BarOS review schedule.
End of Cycle Move the focus subject into permanent review and begin the next two-week focus subject.
