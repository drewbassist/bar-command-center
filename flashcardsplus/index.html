Constitutional Law Card 1 of 100

What level of scrutiny applies to content-based restrictions on speech?

Answer

Strict scrutiny.

Rule

Content-based restrictions on protected speech are constitutional only if they satisfy strict scrutiny.

Notes

Remember that commercial speech and time/place/manner restrictions use different standards.
